Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

SDub Media LLC ("we," "us," "our") operates Slate and Slate Freelance (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, password, phone number, and business information (company name, address).

Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the Service, including gig details, time entries, expenses, invoices, and documents you upload.

Payment Information: Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store your credit card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.

Receipt Images: When you scan receipts, images are processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) to extract text data. Images are not stored by the AI provider after processing.

Location Data: When you use the "Nearby" feature, we use your gig venue's city to find nearby places via Google Maps. We do not track your real-time location.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To process your gigs, expenses, and invoices
  • To generate reports (P&L, tax summaries, mileage)
  • To process payments through Stripe
  • To scan and extract receipt data using AI
  • To send you service-related communications
  • To provide customer support

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL database) with row-level security. Each user's data is isolated — you cannot access another user's data, and they cannot access yours. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

Uploaded files (receipts, documents) are stored in Supabase Storage with authenticated access controls.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Database and authentication
  • Stripe — Payment processing
  • Anthropic (Claude) — AI receipt and document parsing
  • Google Maps — Nearby places and distance calculations
  • Open-Meteo — Weather data
  • GSA.gov — Per diem rate lookups
  • Vercel — Application hosting

Each service has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where required by law (e.g., tax records).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data (invoices, reports)

To exercise these rights, contact us at geoff@sdubmedia.com.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication (keeping you signed in). We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
